Meet the maker

Hi, I’m Lauren! ♡

I started Lauren’s Clay Creations because I wanted to turn my creativity and love for making things into a small business. What started as simply making clay pieces has grown into something I’m really proud of.

I love being able to create unique, handmade pieces that people can enjoy and make their own. As I continue growing my small business, I’m excited to keep creating new designs, learning new techniques, and sharing my work with others.

Thank you for supporting my small business and allowing me to do something I genuinely enjoy!
